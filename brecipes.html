Chocolate Cake

Difficulty: Medium  |  Total Time: 45 mins  |  Makes: 1 Cake

Ingredients

  • 1 cup plain flour
  • 1½ tbsp cocoa powder
  • ½ tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp baking soda
  • 1 cup sugar
  • ½ cup vegetable oil
  • 1 egg
  • ½ cup buttermilk
  • ¼ cup hot coffee

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 175°C.
  2. Mix the dry ingredients together.
  3. Whisk the wet ingredients together.
  4. Combine both mixtures until smooth.
  5. Pour into a greased cake tin.
  6. Bake for 40–45 minutes.
  7. Cool completely before serving.

Chocolate Chip Cookies

Difficulty: Easy  |  Total Time: 30 mins  |  Makes: 12 Cookies

Ingredients

  • 200g unsalted butter
  • 180g brown sugar
  • 70g white sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 240g plain flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 115g chopped chocolate
  • 115g chocolate chips

Method

  1. Cream the butter and sugars.
  2. Add the egg, egg yolk and vanilla.
  3. Mix in the flour, baking soda and salt.
  4. Fold in the chocolate and chocolate chips.
  5. Chill the dough for 2 hours.
  6. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  7. Scoop onto a lined baking tray.
  8. Bake for 11–13 minutes.
  9. Cool for 10 minutes before serving.

Chocolate Chip Muffins

Difficulty: Easy  |  Total Time: 40 mins  |  Makes: 12 Muffins

Ingredients

  • 2 cups plain flour
  • ½ cup sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup milk
  • ½ cup vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 cup chocolate chips

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Mix the flour, sugar, baking powder and salt.
  3. Whisk together the egg, milk, oil and vanilla.
  4. Combine the wet and dry ingredients.
  5. Fold in the chocolate chips.
  6. Fill the muffin cases about ¾ full.
  7. Bake for 18–20 minutes until golden.
  8. Cool before serving.

Cinnamon Rolls

Difficulty: Medium  |  Total Time: 2 Hours  |  Makes: 9 Rolls

Ingredients

  • 3 cups plain flour
  • 2¼ tsp instant yeast
  • ¼ cup sugar
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 cup warm milk
  • ¼ cup melted butter
  • 1 egg
  • ½ cup brown sugar
  • 2 tsp cinnamon powder

Method

  1. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar and salt.
  2. Add the warm milk, butter and egg.
  3. Knead into a smooth dough.
  4. Leave the dough to rise for 1 hour.
  5. Roll out the dough and spread the cinnamon filling.
  6. Roll up tightly and cut into 9 pieces.
  7. Bake at 180°C for 20–25 minutes.
  8. Cool slightly and drizzle with icing.

Lemon Meringue Pie

Difficulty: Hard  |  Total Time: 1 Hour 30 Mins  |  Makes: 1 Pie

Ingredients

  • 1 ready-made pie crust
  • 3 egg yolks
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 2 lemons (juice & zest)
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 3 egg whites
  • ¼ cup caster sugar

Method

  1. Bake the pie crust until lightly golden.
  2. Cook the lemon filling until thick.
  3. Pour the filling into the pie crust.
  4. Whisk the egg whites until soft peaks form.
  5. Add the caster sugar and whisk until stiff peaks form.
  6. Spread the meringue over the lemon filling.
  7. Bake until the meringue is lightly browned.
  8. Cool completely before serving.
