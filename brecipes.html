Chocolate Cake

Ingredients

  • 200g plain flour
  • 200g sugar
  • 50g cocoa powder
  • 2 eggs
  • 120ml milk
  • 120ml vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp baking powder

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Mix all the dry ingredients together.
  3. Add the eggs, milk and oil.
  4. Mix until smooth.
  5. Pour into a cake tin.
  6. Bake for 45 minutes.
  7. Allow the cake to cool before serving.
