Baking Guide

Tips and techniques to help you bake better every time.

Baking Tips

  • Always read the recipe before you start.
  • Use room temperature ingredients for better results.
  • Do not overmix the batter.
  • Check your bake a few minutes before the suggested time.
  • Let your baked goods cool before frosting or serving.

Baking Essentials

Essential Purpose Tips
Measuring Cups Measure dry ingredients like flour and sugar. Spoon the ingredient into the cup and level it off.
Measuring Spoons Measure small amounts of ingredients. Use exact spoons for accuracy.
Mixing Bowl Mix ingredients together. Use a large bowl to avoid spills.
Whisk Whisk eggs, batter and sauces. Whisk in a circular motion.
Oven Bake your creations. Always preheat your oven.
